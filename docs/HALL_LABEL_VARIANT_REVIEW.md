# Hall-label variant review

The accompanying `hall_label_variant_review.csv` contains 2,099 variant records. Status totals are:

| Status | Records |
|---|---:|
| FULLY_VERIFIED | 377 |
| HALL_LIST_CONFIRMED | 772 |
| UNRESOLVED | 950 |
| **Total** | **2,099** |

These counts are a subset of the 3,306 overall records and must not be confused with the overall totals (627 / 1,159 / 1,520). Fourteen spelling pairs were confirmed as separate halls in at least one official session. Do not automatically merge labels such as `MB III 203` and `MB III A203`; preserve the printed hall label and verify against the relevant official document.

See `VSB_OFFICIAL_VERIFICATION_REPORT.md` for evidence scope and limitations.
